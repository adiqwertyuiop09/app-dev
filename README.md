## 🎬 Overview
**Midnight Sun** is a romantic drama film released in **2018**. 
It tells the story of *Katie Price*, a teenage girl who has a rare condition that makes her extremely sensitive to sunlight.

## 👩‍🎤 Main Cast
- **Bella Thorne** as *Katie Price*  
- **Patrick Schwarzenegger** as *Charlie Reed*  
- **Rob Riggle** as *Jack Price* (Katie’s father)

## 💖 Why I Love This Movie
1. It’s emotional and inspiring.  
2. The music is beautiful, especially *“Burn So Bright.”*  
3. It shows how love and courage can overcome fear.
